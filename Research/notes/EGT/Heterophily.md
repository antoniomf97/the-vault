#EGT 

> [!Definition]
> 
> Heterophily is a probability of which individuals of a given species interact with individuals from a different species. When $\hbar=1$, individuals only interact with the ones of different species (maximum heterogeneity). By contrary, when $\hbar=0$, any individual have the same probability to interact with another (minimum heterogeneity). In this last case, we go back to the limit of [[Well-mixed Populations|well-mixed populations]].
