#egt

> [!Definition]
> 
> Homophily a probability of which individuals from different species interact with one another. When $h=1$, individuals only interact with the ones of the same species (maximum heterogeneity). By contrary, when $h=0$, any individual have the same probability to interact with another (minimum heterogeneity). In this last case, we go back to the limit of [[Well-mixed Populations|well-mixed populations]] (assuming heterophily is null).
