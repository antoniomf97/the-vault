#cognition

> [!Definition]
> 
> [*psychology*] Cognitive profile is an individual's pattern of relative strengths and weaknesses across separable cognitive domains (memory, attention, executive function, processing speed, language, visuospatial ability), measured intra-individually rather than against a population mean.


> [!Definition]
> 
> [*mine*] Cognitive profile is a structured characterization of an individual's cognitive abilities, specifically the different types of reasoning.
