**Slide 2**
In today's society, AI is becoming more and more embedded, now posing as social agents. (By July 2025, 10% of all adults in the world were using ChatGPT)
As such, humans and AI share systems but not necessarily the same interests, and this creates a social dilemma. This is not the best environment for cooperation to flourish.
The decisions that any agent takes in any interaction will depend on how they reason.
And over time, these interactions will change not only how humans reason but also how they themselves reason.
