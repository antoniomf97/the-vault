## What the field has established

In evolutionary game theory, researchers have been studying the cooperative effects, not just to generally unravel the cooperation conundrum, but also to understand some specific social mechanics, such as delegation, fairness, emotions, and others.

## What has been tried on cognition

Whereas most works on cooperation dynamics focus on social learning, assuming individuals are purely rational [], many other works have been done in exploring different types of reasoning.
